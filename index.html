AI Engineering — From First LLM Call to a Grounded RAG Product

A build-first course for experienced software engineers moving into AI engineering. Every lesson ends in something you run: a call, a service, a graph, a pipeline, or a guarded agent. Start at lesson 1 and follow the “What's next” link at the bottom of each page.

If you are new here Read Lesson 1 first. It maps the six-layer stack and marks which layers your existing software skills already cover. The skill map is the one-page version of the whole course.
